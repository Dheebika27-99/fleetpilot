# General service schedule

*Sample policy for the fictional company FleetPilot Logistics. Not manufacturer guidance.*

| Item | Diesel trucks and vans | Hybrids | Electric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Engine oil and filter | every 15,000 km or 12 months | every 15,000 km or 12 months | not needed |
| Coolant check | monthly | monthly | every 3 months (battery cooling) |
| Brake inspection | every 20,000 km | every 30,000 km | every 30,000 km |
| Full service | every 12 months | every 12 months | every 12 months |

## Vehicles with no service record
A vehicle with an empty `last_service` date has no recorded service. It must be booked
for a full service within **14 days**, regardless of mileage.

## Older vehicles
Vehicles older than **10 years** get a full service every **6 months** instead of 12.
