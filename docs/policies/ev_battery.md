# Electric and hybrid battery policy

*Sample policy for the fictional company FleetPilot Logistics. Not manufacturer guidance.*

Applies to the bZ4X, eCanter (electric) and Prius, Dutro hybrid vehicles.

## Daily charging
- Charge electric vehicles overnight at the depot to **80 to 90 %** for daily use.
- Charge to 100 % only before unusually long routes.
- Avoid leaving a vehicle below **10 %** for more than a few hours.

## BATTERY_LOW warning
1. Electric vehicles: drive to the nearest charger or return to the depot. Do not start a new delivery.
2. Hybrid vehicles: the engine will charge the battery; report it if the warning repeats on the same day.

## Battery health
- The battery health report is reviewed every **6 months**.
- A vehicle whose range drops by more than **20 %** compared to new is scheduled for a battery check.
