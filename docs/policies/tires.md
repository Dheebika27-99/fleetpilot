# Tire policy

*Sample policy for the fictional company FleetPilot Logistics. Not manufacturer guidance.*

## Pressure checks
- Drivers check tire pressure visually before every shift.
- A full pressure check with a gauge is done **weekly**, with tires cold.
- Electric vehicles are heavier and wear tires faster: check them **twice a week**.

## LOW_TIRE_PRESSURE warning
1. Pull over safely at the next safe place.
2. Check all tires visually for damage or nails.
3. Inflate to the pressure on the door label. If the pressure drops again within 24 hours, book a puncture repair.
4. Never continue driving on a visibly flat tire.

## Replacement
- Replace tires at **3 mm** tread depth (stricter than the legal minimum).
- Rotate tires every **10,000 km** on vans and trucks.
